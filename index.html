About me

I’m a third-year Civil Engineering student at San Diego State University, expecting to graduate in 2028. I’m currently interning at Moment Engineering, where I work in the lab and out in the field.

That interest has grown into projects like designing the floor plan and office space for Moment Engineering.

Rugby and sports in general have been a big part of my life and have shaped the person I am today.
